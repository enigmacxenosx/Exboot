## How we work

Each person is accountable for observable outcomes within their role, not only a title. The team coordinates around building, acquiring, activating, retaining, and monetizing ENOSX AI users while supporting the wider Enosx Technologies portfolio. Enosh sets company priorities and resolves major company-wide decisions; role owners make decisions within their agreed scope and report measurable progress. Work that crosses roles or is not covered by a role charter must have a clearly named owner and next step in the relevant project issue or decision record.

## Operating cadence

- **Daily founder coordination:** Enosh and Vince use a short check-in to confirm the founder's top priorities, meetings, follow-ups, decisions needed, blockers, and end-of-day status. This is a coordination rhythm, not a substitute for team-wide planning.
- **Weekly team sync:** Review the scorecard against targets, the week's primary outcome, role updates, user/customer feedback, decisions, blockers, risks, and next-week commitments. Record an owner and due date for every commitment. Use the [ENOSX weekly team sync template](https://github.com/enigmacxenosx/enosxai/blob/main/docs/ENOSX_Weekly_Team_Sync_Template.md).
- **Escalation:** Raise security or personal-data issues, payment incidents, legal commitments, public statements, access changes, customer incidents, and at-risk deadlines promptly with Enosh and the relevant owner.

## Scope and privacy

This file records public-safe roles and responsibilities only. Do not add private contact details, compensation, credentials, confidential customer information, or unapproved employment terms. Repository access, code merges, production configuration, payments, legal commitments, and public company statements require approval from the appropriate owner.
